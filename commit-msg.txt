chore: relicense from MIT to Apache-2.0

Apache-2.0 carries an explicit patent grant (section 3) and contribution terms
(section 5), neither of which MIT addresses at all. For a plugin an enterprise
might adopt, that difference matters more than MIT's brevity, and it matches the
other plugins in this ecosystem.

The licence text is the official file from apache.org, not a transcription: the
download's SHA-256 is asserted before anything is written, and only the
appendix's [yyyy] [name of copyright owner] placeholder is filled in. The leading
blank line the canonical file carries is dropped so the file begins at the title,
which is the form the sibling repositories use.

publish-check now asserts Apache's shape instead of MIT's - including that the
appendix placeholder was actually replaced, since shipping it verbatim leaves the
copyright holder unnamed. That assertion was verified by restoring the
placeholder and watching the check fail.
